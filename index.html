How it works

Each subscriber receives a personal tap tag and their own Overseer icon, both registered to their account. When something doesn't feel right, they tap the tag with their phone, or tap or click the icon, and choose what they need:

Send a screenshot

Share a picture of a suspicious message so our team can review it and tell you how urgent it is.

Describe what's happening

Type a short description of a call or situation that seems off, and our team will follow up.

Call a caregiver

Reach a family member or trusted contact quickly.

Call 911

In an emergency, one tap connects to emergency services.

Physical and virtual protection. One price.

Every account has two layers of protection: a physical tag you can hold and virtual protection on every screen you use. Help is within reach wherever you are, at the kitchen table, in your favorite chair, or at the computer.

Physical protection

Your protective tap tag

A small tag registered just to you. Keep it by the phone or wherever you need it, and tap it with your phone to reach a real person in seconds. No app, no password to remember.

Virtual protection

Your Overseer icon on every device

A gold Overseer icon on your phone, tablet, and computer that opens the same help page with one tap or click. We'll help you set it up on each device you use.

Both are included with every account, at no extra cost. One subscription, protection in your hand and on your screens.

Real people when it matters

Our system helps sort incoming requests by urgency, but any situation involving money is always escalated to a live person on our team. We never ask for passwords, bank account numbers, or payment information over text.

We will never access, or ask for access to, your device. No one from The Overseer's Touch will ask you to install an app, share your screen, or let us into your phone or computer. If someone claiming to be us asks, hang up. It's a scam.

Who we serve

The Overseer's Touch is designed for older adults and the family members and caregivers who look out for them. Service is offered through a monthly subscription.

Text messages: Subscribers and their designated caregivers may receive account and service text messages from The Overseer's Touch. See our Terms of Service, SMS Terms, and Privacy Policy.
