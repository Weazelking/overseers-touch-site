A trusted second opinion, one tap away.

The Overseer's Touch helps seniors pause and check before responding to a suspicious call, text, email, or pop-up, and keeps their family in the loop.

How it works

Each subscriber receives a personal tap tag registered to their account. When something doesn't feel right, they tap the tag with their phone and choose what they need:

Send a screenshot

Share a picture of a suspicious message so our team can review it and tell you how urgent it is.

Describe what's happening

Type a short description of a call or situation that seems off, and our team will follow up.

Call a caregiver

Reach a family member or trusted contact quickly.

Call 911

In an emergency, one tap connects to emergency services.

Real people when it matters

Our system helps sort incoming requests by urgency, but any situation involving money is always escalated to a live person on our team. We never ask for passwords, bank account numbers, or payment information over text.

Who we serve

The Overseer's Touch is designed for older adults and the family members and caregivers who look out for them. Service is offered through a monthly subscription.

Text messages: Subscribers and their designated caregivers may receive account and service text messages from The Overseer's Touch. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.
